# WJBKZD Skills

Reusable, environment-neutral AI agent skills.

## Included skills

- `pledge-risk-research` — A-share stock-pledge risk research with auditable evidence, financial-quality checks, pledge-specific stress analysis, and optional verified Obsidian delivery.

## Privacy and portability

The published skills do not contain personal usernames, local absolute paths, Vault locations, email addresses, access tokens, or cookies. Environment-specific paths and data providers are resolved at runtime.

## Installation

Copy a skill directory into the skills folder supported by your AI coding or research agent. Review the skill's `SKILL.md` and ensure any required data source is authorized before use.

Financial outputs are research aids and do not constitute investment advice.
